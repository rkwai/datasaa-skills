# datasaa-client-week

## Monday: plan
- Pull open items from status, the client brief's open questions, and dates.
- Draft: this week's outcomes, what we need from the client, and the risks.

## Mid-week: track
- Check each planned outcome against the records. Moving, blocked, or done.
- If blocked on the client, draft the ask. A person approves before it's sent.

## End of day: meeting scan
- Read the day's client meetings and threads.
- Send new material to the context agent's intake.
- Flag anything that changes dates or scope.

## Don't
Act like a program manager. Program-level items get tagged to their human owner and stop there.
